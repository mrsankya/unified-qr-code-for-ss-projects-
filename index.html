What is SAS Projects & Tech Solutions?

SAS Projects & Tech Solutions is an advanced digital agency co-founded by Sanket Bhende and Sakshi Patil, crafting high-conversion 3D web experiences, smart table-to-cloud QR ecosystems, and custom full-stack solutions.

Who are the founders and their roles?

Sakshi Patil is the Co-Founder & Tech Specialist (CSE & Software Architecture, +91 95794 53006). Sanket Bhende is the Founder & Web Architect (Full-Stack & 3D Web Creative Developer, +91 98227 25265).

Where do you provide tech solutions and websites?

Headquartered in Jalgaon & Pune, Maharashtra, we build and deliver websites, ERP dashboards, AI automation, and academic projects across all cities in India (Mumbai, Nashik, Nagpur, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, etc.) and worldwide remotely (USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia).

Do you develop Engineering & BTech Final Year Projects (CSE)?

Yes, we engineer production-grade BTech/BE final year CSE projects, minor projects, hackathon prototypes, hardware-software integration, and technical project reports with complete source code and deployment assistance.

What software & AI solutions do you build for businesses & MSMEs?

We build custom web applications, React.js & Node.js software, custom ERPs, inventory dashboards, table-to-cloud Smart QR ordering hubs, generative AI tools, and automated business workflows.
